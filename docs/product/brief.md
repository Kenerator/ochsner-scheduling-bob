# Product brief

Updated 2026-10-09. BOB helps people inspect, correct and explicitly confirm
synthetic scheduling choices without losing control of identity, consent or
uncertain effects. It reconciles accepted assignment behavior into one core,
not a merger of the original four PoCs or a clinical assistant.

Seven pinned [persona hypotheses](personas.md) inform concise numbered choices,
recoverable public/private corrections, no email prerequisite and truthful
paired support. [Native stories](../../specs/001-ochsner-scheduling-assistant/spec.md)
own acceptance detail; names/model choices confer no permissions.

The local Marimo and CLI paths use unchanged supplied HTTP fixtures, real ZEN
guards and public-only typo suggestions. Optional language proposes bounded
public intent, never identity or consent. Unknown writes retain durable local
protection through reset/restart. Diagnostics and replay are bounded synthetic
inspection, not live transaction replay or full renderer parity.

Evidence, limitations and priorities are linked in [decisions](decisions.md),
[native tasks](../../specs/001-ochsner-scheduling-assistant/tasks.md) and
[next steps](next-steps.md). No real PHI/API, production authentication,
live staff delivery, deployment, branding endorsement or certification is claimed.
